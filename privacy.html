Your Privacy Matters

At AquaCare, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you share with us.

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

01

Introduction

Welcome to AquaCare. We provide aquarium setup, maintenance, consultation and related aquarium care services.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect information when you visit our website or contact us regarding our services.

02

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide when you use our website or request our services.

Information you provide

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Aquarium location
  • Preferred visit date and time
  • Information about your aquarium or service requirements

Information collected automatically

When you visit our website, certain technical information may be collected automatically, depending on the technologies and services used by the website.

This may include browser type, device information, approximate location, pages visited and general website usage information.

03

How We Use Your Information

Information provided to AquaCare may be used to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and requests.
  • Schedule aquarium visits and consultations.
  • Provide aquarium setup and maintenance services.
  • Understand your aquarium requirements.
  • Communicate with you about requested services.
  • Improve our website and customer experience.
  • Protect our website from misuse or unauthorized activity.
04

Booking and Visit Requests

When you submit a booking or visit request, we may use the information you provide to understand your requirements and contact you regarding the requested service.

Information such as your name, phone number, email address, aquarium location, preferred date and service requirements may be used for this purpose.

05

Sharing of Information

AquaCare does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information with service providers or technology providers when reasonably necessary to operate our website, communicate with you or provide requested services.

We may also disclose information when required by applicable law, regulation, legal process or to protect the rights and safety of AquaCare, our customers or others.

06

You can control or restrict cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functionality.

07

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information provided to us from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

08

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining business records and meeting applicable legal obligations.

09

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights regarding the personal information we hold about you.

You may contact us to request information about the personal data we hold, request correction of inaccurate information, or ask questions about how your information is used.

10

11

We do not knowingly request personal information from children through our website.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, website functionality or applicable requirements.
