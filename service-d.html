WHAT GOES INTO A PROPER SETUP

A Strong Foundation for a Healthy Aquarium.

Every successful aquarium starts with the right foundation. We carefully plan each stage to create an environment that looks beautiful and supports healthy aquatic life.

01
STEP ONE

Planning

We understand your space, aquarium size, placement, style, and overall vision before the setup begins.

02
STEP TWO

Equipment

03
