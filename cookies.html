COOKIES POLICY

Cookies & Your Privacy

This policy explains how AquaCare may use cookies and similar technologies to provide, improve and understand our website experience.

Last Updated: September 15, 2026

01

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may store on your device when you visit them.

They can help websites remember information, understand how visitors use the website and provide certain functionality.

02

How AquaCare Uses Cookies

AquaCare may use cookies and similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • Helping the website function correctly.
  • Remembering selected website preferences.
  • Supporting RTL and dark mode preferences.
  • Understanding general website usage.
  • Improving website performance and user experience.
  • Identifying technical issues and improving website reliability.
03

Types of Cookies

Cookies used on a website can generally be grouped into different categories depending on their purpose.

Essential Cookies

These cookies may be necessary for basic website functionality. Without them, certain features may not operate correctly.

Preference Cookies

These cookies or similar storage technologies may remember preferences such as language, RTL settings or display preferences.

Analytics Cookies

Where analytics services are used, cookies may help us understand general website traffic and how visitors interact with our pages.

Functional Cookies

These technologies may support features that improve the functionality and usability of the website.

04

Cookies We May Use

Depending on the features and services enabled on the AquaCare website, cookies or local browser storage may be used to remember certain preferences.

Type Purpose
Essential Supports basic website functionality.
Preferences Remembers selected website preferences.
Analytics Helps understand general website usage.
Functional Supports enhanced website features.
05

Local Storage and Website Preferences

Some AquaCare website preferences may be stored locally in your browser rather than through traditional cookies.

For example, your selected RTL or dark mode preference may be remembered so that the website can provide a consistent experience when you return.

06

Third-Party Cookies

Some third-party services used by a website may place their own cookies or similar technologies on your device.

These services may include analytics, embedded content, security tools or other technologies used to operate and improve the website.

Third-party cookies are controlled by the respective third-party provider and may be subject to that provider's own privacy policy.

07

Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • View cookies stored on your device.
  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block cookies from specific websites.
  • Block third-party cookies.
  • Configure your browser to ask before storing certain cookies.

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the availability or functionality of some website features.

08

Cookie Duration

Session Cookies

Session cookies are generally removed when your browsing session ends.

Persistent Cookies

Persistent cookies remain on your device for a defined period or until they are deleted through your browser settings.

09

Your Choices

You can choose how your browser handles cookies and similar technologies.

You may also clear stored website data from your browser if you no longer want previously saved preferences to remain on your device.

Some website functions may not work as intended if certain technologies are disabled.

10

Privacy and Personal Information

Cookies and similar technologies may sometimes be associated with information that can be considered personal information.

How AquaCare handles personal information is explained in our Privacy Policy .

Any updated version will be published on this page together with a revised "Last Updated" date.
